008 / A few good questions

Before we build,
let’s get clear.

Good partnerships start with precise questions. Here are the conversations we usually recommend having first.

Can you join an existing product team?+

Yes. We can explore a defined feature scope, API work or an engineering extension that fits your stack, repository and review process.

Can AI work with our current business systems?+

Often. The first step is to map your data sources, permissions and workflow. Then we can assess APIs, retrieval, automations and where human review belongs.

Do you only work with .NET?+

No. Our work can span ASP.NET Core, C#, Angular, React, MERN, SQL databases, REST APIs and cloud-connected solutions according to the project's requirements.

Can a management system be customised?+

Yes. Inventory, education, healthcare, CRM, HR, retail and manufacturing solutions are presented as configurable concepts. We start with the processes and reports your team needs.

How would a new project begin?+

Share the problem, goals, current systems and rough timeline. We can then discuss discovery, an initial scope, milestones, communication expectations and the right delivery approach.
