Common questions

Answers before the first meeting.

Can AI be added to our existing software?

Often yes, if there are safe integration points and a clear use case. We first review the APIs, data security needs and how users should verify results.

Do AI automations need human approval?

For higher-risk tasks, approval and audit trails are important. We design review checkpoints based on the actual decision and its consequences.

Can you use n8n with our CRM and internal tools?

We can evaluate n8n and API-based workflows for supported services, credentials management, rate limits and reliable retries.
